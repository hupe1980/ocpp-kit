must agree on, the same category as the action names the vendored schemas already carry. The
appendix's prose descriptions are not copied and the appendix itself is not redistributed.

## Known gap

OCA published **OCPP 2.0.1 edition 4** and **OCPP 2.1 edition 2** on 2025-12-04, with errata
in 2026-04. The schemas vendored here are older — 2.0.1 FINAL and 2.1 edition 1 — as the table
above records.

Closing that gap is a mechanical, reviewable change: drop the new schemas into
`schemas/<version>/`, run `cargo xtask codegen`, read the diff, and update this file. The
schema conformance suite validates the new types against the new schemas automatically, and
the action-count assertions in the generator will flag any action that appeared or vanished.

## Published-schema defects handled explicitly

Two 1.6 schema defects are worked around in the generator rather than in the types, with the
workaround declared rather than implicit:

* `MeterValues` lists `Hertz` in its unit enumeration; `StopTransaction` does not. The
  generated `UnitOfMeasure` is the union, declared in the generator's `V16_UNION_ENUMS` list.
  A `StopTransaction` carrying `Hertz` would be rejected by the published `StopTransaction`
  schema even though it is a legal `UnitOfMeasure`.
* `MeterValue.sampledValue` is `minItems: 1` under `MeterValues` and unbounded under
  `StopTransaction`. The tighter bound wins, matching the specification's own cardinality
  table.
